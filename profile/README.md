<h3 align="center">Trade show prospecting for European B2B teams.</h3>

<p align="center">
  DataOrigin finds the companies exhibiting at European B2B trade shows, ranks them by your Ideal Customer Profile and gives you the people to contact before the fair.
</p>

### How it works

1. **Pick the fair.** Search the calendar of European B2B trade shows by sector, country and date.
2. **See who exhibits.** Get the list of exhibitors with web, LinkedIn, sector, size and country.
3. **Rank by your ICP.** Each company gets a score from your target industries, countries and company size.
4. **Find the decision makers.** Look up the people by role and export the list to your CRM or a spreadsheet.

[Explore the platform](https://dataorigin.es/en/) or [book a demo](https://dataorigin.es/en/contact-us/).

### Free tools

- [Trade show calendar](https://dataorigin.es/en/trade-show-calendar/): European B2B fairs with dates, venues and sectors.
- [Company trade show lookup](https://dataorigin.es/en/tools/company-trade-show-lookup/): enter a company website and see the B2B fairs where it has exhibited.
- [Trade show ROI calculator](https://dataorigin.es/en/tools/trade-show-roi-calculator/): estimate the return of a fair before you book the stand.
- [Lead Scanner app](https://dataorigin.es/en/tools/trade-show-lead-scanner/): scan badges and business cards at the booth (Android).
- [Browser extension](https://dataorigin.es/en/browser-extension/) for Chrome and Firefox.
- [B2B trade show statistics](https://dataorigin.es/en/blog/b2b-trade-show-statistics/), updated with sources and our own exhibitor data.
